# Enough for All / 足りる経済へ

Live site: <https://tamorist.github.io/enough/>

A single page on the economic roots of the climate crisis and the direction of change it calls for: how fast population, emissions and atmospheric CO₂ have grown in the last 50 years; how unequal wealth and unequal emissions overlap; Earth Overshoot Day, “how many Earths” by country and planetary boundaries; emissions by sector; fossil fuels still ~80% of energy with renewables added on top; the limits of green growth; and why we need to redistribute wealth and leave the overconsumption economy. Our own estimates are labelled as such, and every figure is listed in the Sources section.

- `index.html` — the whole site (Japanese / English)
- `og.png` / `og-en.png` — share images, Japanese / English (1200×630)

Sister sites: [Before the Tipping Point](https://tamorist.github.io/tipping-point/) · [The Slow War](https://tamorist.github.io/slow-war/) · [83 Billion Reasons](https://tamorist.github.io/83-billion/) · All sites: [ClimateWatchMan](https://tamorist.github.io/)

気候変動の社会的・経済的な原因と、必要な社会変革の方向性をまとめたサイトです。この50年の人口・排出・大気中の CO₂ の急増、重なり合う富と排出の偏り、アース・オーバーシュート・デーと国別の「地球何個分」、地球の限界、部門別の排出、いまも約8割が化石燃料で再エネは「上乗せ」になっていること、グリーン成長の限界、そして富を分け直し過剰消費の経済から抜け出す必要がある理由を、出典つきで示しています。
